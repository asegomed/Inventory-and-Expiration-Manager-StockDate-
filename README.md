# StockDate: Inventory and Expiration Manager

**StockDate** is a web application designed to solve a critical problem: the inefficient management of inventory and expiration dates in small businesses and local food banks[cite: 3]. Its main objective is to drastically reduce food waste and optimize the time of volunteers and coordinators[cite: 3].

## What are we developing? (Project Scope & Introduction)
Currently, the project is in its initial planning and MVP (Minimum Viable Product) development phase. What we are going to build includes:
* **CRUD System:** Complete management of creation, reading, update, and deletion of inventory batches[cite: 3].
* **Control Panel (Dashboard):** Automatic visual alerts to identify products nearing their expiration date at a glance[cite: 3].
* **Waste Reports:** Generation of analytical reports to optimize purchasing or donation decisions and minimize waste[cite: 3].
* **Role-Based Access Control:** Customized views depending on the user profile (worker or client/beneficiary)[cite: 3].
